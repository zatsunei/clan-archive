<h2>Character over skill.</h2>
<p>Worth is inherent, not defined by skill, but revealed through character. We believe that every person has inherent worth, independent of their abilities or their skill. We believe that while skill may determine what a person can do, character is what truly matters to us.</p>

<br><br>
<h2>Strength through unity.</h2>
<p>We believe a clan is strongest when it is united. Individual skill and ability have value, but cooperation allows it to become something greater. We seek to support one another, overcome disagreements, and work towards a common purpose rather than letting personal interests divide us.</p>

<br><br>
<h2>With great power comes great responsibility.</h2>
<p>Authority is not a privilege to be used for personal gain, but a responsibility entrusted to those who hold it. The greater one's rank, influence, or power is within the Clan, the greater their responsibility is toward its members.</p>

<p>Leaders are expected to act with fairness, integrity, and restraint. Those entrusted with authority should use it to serve the clan, not themselves.</p>

<br><br>
<h2>Lead by example.</h2>
<p>High-ranks should not demand standards from others that they don't follow themselves. Those who lead the Clan should embody the standards they expect from its members and should never demand from others what they would not do themselves.</p>

<p>The conduct of a leader influences those around them. Therefore, we expect our high-ranking members to demonstrate discipline, integrity, respect, responsibility, and humility in all their actions. Leaders should acknowledge their mistakes and learn from them, uphold the same standards they enforce, and set an example which is worthy of being followed</p>

<br><br>
<h2>Humility before pride.</h2>
<p>We believe that pride, when allowed to become arrogance, can lead to the downfall of both individuals and our Clan. Success, rank, skill, and recognition should never lead us to believe we are above others or incapable of failure.</p>

<p>We value humility and self-awareness. We should be willing to recognize our weaknesses, accept constructive criticism, learn from our mistakes, and acknowledge the abilities of others.</p>

<br><br>
<h2>Iron sharpens iron.</h2>
<p>We grow stronger through one another. The improvement of a member shall not be solely their own responsibility, but the responsibility of the Clan. Members should challenge, teach, and encourage each other to improve, while accepting constructive criticism and learning from their mistakes.</p>
