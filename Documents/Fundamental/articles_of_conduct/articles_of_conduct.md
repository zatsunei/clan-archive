<h3>Z A T S U N E I .</h3>

<br>

</div>

<h2 align="center">Article 1. ― Loyalty and Allegiance</h2>

<div align="center">

The clan member shall place loyalty to the Clan above all else. Betrayal is illegal.

</div>

<br>
<h2 align="center">Article 2. ― Camaraderie and Respect</h2>

<div align="center">

Between clan members there shall be respect and companionship. Betrayal of one another is treacherous.

</div>

<br>
<h2 align="center">Article 3. ― Secrecy</h2>

<div align="center">

The affairs of the Clan are not to be shared outside it.

</div>

<br>
<h2 align="center">Article 4. ― Obedience</h2>

<div align="center">

The rank within the Clan is to be respected. The word of the high-ranking member holds authority over those beneath them.

</div>

<br>
<h2 align="center">Article 5. ― Honor and Reputation</h2>

<div align="center">

The name of the Clan is carried in the conduct of its clan members. The dishonor of a member is the dishonor of the Clan. To uphold the honor of the Clan is the duty of every member.

</div>

<br>
<h2 align="center">Article 6. ― Discipline</h2>

<div align="center">

A clan member shall stay disciplined, for the strength of the Clan relies on the discipline of its members. Neglect of oneself shall not take place.

</div>

<br>
<h2 align="center">Article 7. ― Outsiders and Allies</h2>

<div align="center">

Allies of the Clan are to be treated with respect and honesty. Those outside the Clan are owed neither loyalty nor secrecy, only hospitality.

</div>

<br>
<h2 align="center">Article 8. ― Incitement of Conflict, War and Peace</h2>

<div align="center">

No clan member shall incite conflict with those outside the Clan. Only the Archon General or the Marshal may take such action. A clan member may engage in combat in the name of the Clan, but the declaration of war or peace is not theirs to make.

</div>

<br>
<h2 align="center">Article 9. ― Justice</h2>

<div align="center">

Disputes among members shall be judged with fairness and impartiality, not with favoritism. The rank shall not shield one from justice. Disputes within the Clan shall be judged by the Archon General or the Marshal, except where further law provides otherwise.

</div>

<br>
<h2 align="center">Article 10. ― Consequence and Punishment</h2>

<div align="center">

A member who breaks the laws of the Clan shall be judged for it. The sentence shall fit the transgression.

</div>

<br>
<h2 align="center">Article 11. ― Growth</h2>

<div align="center">

The Clan grows through those who choose to build it further. None shall be compelled to do so, but those who do shall be honored among the Clan.

</div>

<br>
<h2 align="center">Article 12. ― Lawfulness outside the Clan</h2>

<div align="center">

A clan member shall not break the laws beyond the Clan. Defiance against them brings shame upon us, unless such defiance is dealt to our enemies.

</div>

<br>
<h2 align="center">Article 13. ― Restraint and Humility</h2>

<div align="center">

A clan member shall not let anger dictate their actions and pride shall not dictate their worth. Restraint and humility are what they should demonstrate.

</div>

<br>
<h2 align="center">Article 14. ― Gratitude</h2>

<div align="center">

A clan member shall be grateful for what they have and what they receive. They must not take it for granted.

</div>

<br>
<h2 align="center">Article 15. ― Endurance</h2>

<div align="center">

A clan member shall endure hardship and not abandon the Clan for it.

</div>
