<h2 align="center">Article 1. ― Loyalty and Allegiance</h2>

<div align="center">

The clan member shall place loyalty to the Clan above all else. Betrayal is illegal.

</div>

<br>
<h2 align="center">Article 2. ― Camaraderie and Respect</h2>

<div align="center">

Between clan members there shall be respect and companionship. Betrayal of one another is treacherous.

</div>

<br>
<h2 align="center">Article 3. ― Secrecy</h2>

<div align="center">

The affairs of the Clan are not to be shared outside it.

</div>

<br>
<h2 align="center">Article 4. ― Obedience</h2>

<div align="center">

The rank within the Clan is to be respected. The word of the high-ranking member holds authority over those beneath them.

</div>

<br>
<h2 align="center">Article 5. ― Honor and Reputation</h2>

<div align="center">

The name of the Clan is carried in the conduct of its clan members. The dishonor of a member is the dishonor of the Clan. To uphold the honor of the Clan is the duty of every member.

</div>

<br>
<h2 align="center">Article 6. ― Discipline</h2>

<div align="center">

A clan member shall stay disciplined, for the strength of the Clan relies on the discipline of its members. Neglect of oneself shall not take place.

</div>
